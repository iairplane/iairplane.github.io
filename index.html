Research, systems, and benchmarks

IROS 2026 · First-listed co-first author

RoboHarness: A memory-augmented policy harness for VLA robustness

RoboHarness adapts frozen vision-language-action policies through dual-memory retrieval and attribution-driven interventions. Across three VLA backbones, it improves average success by 56.6 percentage points, including 89.1 points on long-horizon tasks.

ProjectPaperCode

Ongoing research · Co-author

ManiLadder: Benchmarking robot manipulation through a categorized, multi-level task ladder

ManiLadder organizes more than 100 simulated manipulation tasks into four difficulty levels, with one workflow for task construction, demonstrations, policy training, and evaluation. The benchmark makes it easier to see where capability changes as tasks demand longer horizons, richer coordination, and stronger generalization.

ICML 2026 regular paper · Co-author

GKD-Recruiter: Graph knowledge distillation for spatial crowdsourcing

GKD-Recruiter combines directional social influence with worker–task affinity and uses graph knowledge distillation plus Rainbow DQN to recruit workers for the right physical tasks under limited demand.

ProjectPaperCode

ECML PKDD 2025 · First author

DRNCS: Dual-level route generation through contraction and shortcuts

DRNCS builds a sparse road graph with shortcut-edge differential contraction, then generates routes on the contracted structure. It reports 0.3–0.8 ms per trip and more than 40% faster queries across five real-world datasets.

Theoretical Computer Science 1054 (2025) · Co-author

Algorithms for the Shortest Path Tour Problem

Two exact, linear-space algorithms solve ordered waypoint tours on road networks. Stage Dijkstra reaches the best-known time bound, while Global Dijkstra is faster in practice on large graphs.
