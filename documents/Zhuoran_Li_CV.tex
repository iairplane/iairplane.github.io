\experience{Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)}{Jun. 2024 -- Present}
{Undergraduate Researcher $\vert$ Advisors: Profs. Xiaofeng Gao, Jinyu Gu, and Haibo Chen}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{RoboHarness: Memory-Augmented Policy Harness for VLA Robustness} --- Built an inference-time
  reliability layer for frozen VLA policies using contrastive Dual-Memory retrieval and attribution-guided
  interventions; raised average success by 56.6 percentage points across three backbones. \textit{IROS 2026.}
  \item \textbf{OpenHarmony Embodied Agent Systems} --- Secured a nationally selected RMB 100,000 NSFC
  undergraduate research grant as Student PI and led research on embodied-agent systems for OpenHarmony.
  \item \textbf{GKD-Recruiter} --- Framed spatial recruitment as heterogeneous multi-agent task allocation,
  matching agent capabilities and social influence to task demands through graph knowledge distillation
  and Rainbow DQN. \textit{ICML 2026.}
  \item \textbf{DRNCS} --- Built a hierarchical route-generation method for navigation agents on dense road
  graphs; node contraction and shortcut injection reduced planning to 0.3--0.8 ms per route and
  3,356 routes/s. \textit{ECML PKDD 2025.}
  \item \textbf{Shortest Path Tour Problem} --- Implemented and benchmarked exact Stage and Global Dijkstra
  planners for agents visiting ordered waypoint sets, reaching up to a 50$\times$ speedup on million-node
  road networks. \textit{Theoretical Computer Science, 2025.}
\end{itemize}

  four calibrated difficulty levels spanning diverse embodiments and manipulation skills.
